Max Verstappen

About Max

Max Verstappen is a Dutch Formula 1 driver who races for Red Bull Racing. Born in Belgium in 1997, he started karting at a young age and quickly progressed through the racing categories before reaching Formula 1.

Why Max Verstappen is interesting

  • He became the youngest driver to compete in Formula 1.
  • He won his first Formula 1 World Championship in 2021.
  • He is known for his aggressive driving style, speed, and consistency.

Max's Career

  1. Started racing in karting at a young age.
  2. Moved to Formula 3 in 2014.
  3. Made his Formula 1 debut with Toro Rosso in 2015.
  4. Joined Red Bull Racing in 2016 and became a multiple Formula 1 World Champion.
