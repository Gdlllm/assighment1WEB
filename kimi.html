Kimi Antonelli

About Kimi

Kimi Antonelli is an Italian Formula 1 driver who races for Mercedes. Born in Bologna in 2006, he quickly progressed through junior racing categories before reaching Formula 1.

Why Kimi Antonelli is interesting

  • He made his Formula 1 debut for Mercedes in 2025.
  • He became one of the youngest drivers on the F1 grid.
  • He showed strong pace and potential in his early career.

Kimi's Career

  1. Started racing in karting at a young age.
  2. Won titles in Formula 4 and Formula Regional.
  3. Moved directly to Formula 2 after Formula Regional.
  4. Joined Mercedes Formula 1 team in 2025.
