Kimi Antonelli

Andrea Kimi Antonelli is an Italian racing driver who made his Formula 1 debut with Mercedes in 2025. He progressed through karting and junior single-seater championships before reaching F1.

About Kimi

Born in Bologna, Italy, on 25 August 2006, Antonelli became known for his rapid progress through the junior racing categories. His driving style is associated with confidence and speed.

Career highlights

  • Developed his skills in karting from an early age.
  • Won titles in Formula 4 and Formula Regional competition.
  • Moved to Formula 2 before stepping up to Formula 1.
  • Joined Mercedes for his Formula 1 debut in 2025.

Team members: Dinmukhamed and Assylzhan
